#Zero fear — Product Vision

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Initial Draft  
**Product:** Zero fear Sri Lanka

## 1. Product Overview

 Zero fear is an AI-powered electric vehicle trip-planning mobile application designed specifically for Sri Lanka.

It helps EV owners plan journeys, discover charging stations, estimate battery consumption, identify suitable charging stops and reduce range anxiety.

## 2. Problem Statement

Electric vehicle owners travelling across Sri Lanka face several challenges:

- Uncertainty about battery consumption during long-distance journeys.
- Difficulty finding compatible charging stations.
- Lack of reliable charging station information.
- Difficulty estimating charging costs.
- Range anxiety when travelling through unfamiliar areas and mountainous terrain.

## 3. Proposed Solution

EV Navigator provides an intelligent trip-planning experience using vehicle specifications, road routes, elevation information and charging station data.

The platform combines geographical information, energy prediction algorithms and AI-powered assistance to help EV owners make better travel decisions.

## 4. Target Audience

- Private electric vehicle owners in Sri Lanka.
- Long-distance EV travellers.
- EV fleet operators (future expansion).

## 5. Core Value Proposition

Enable EV owners to travel across Sri Lanka with greater confidence through vehicle-specific energy predictions and intelligent charging recommendations.

## 6. Initial Platform

Android mobile application built using React Native and Expo, with iOS support planned using the same codebase.

## 7. Product Vision

To become a trusted intelligent mobility platform for electric vehicle owners in Sri Lanka.

## 8. Initial Product Goals

- Simplify EV journey planning.
- Improve charging station discovery.
- Predict vehicle energy consumption.
- Recommend suitable charging stops.
- Provide AI-powered travel assistance.

## 9. Long-Term Vision

Expand into a comprehensive EV ecosystem connecting drivers, charging station operators, businesses and EV service providers.
