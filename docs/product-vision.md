# EV Navigator – Product Vision

**Product Name:** EV Navigator  
**Product Category:** Intelligent Electric Vehicle Ecosystem  
**Initial Target Market:** Sri Lanka  
**Initial Platform:** Android  
**Future Platform:** iOS  

---

## 1. Product Overview

EV Navigator is an intelligent electric vehicle ecosystem designed to simplify and improve the complete EV ownership and driving experience.

The platform aims to bring together intelligent trip planning, charging station discovery, personalized vehicle intelligence, AI-powered dashboard scanning, eco-driving rewards, vehicle comparisons and an electric vehicle marketplace.

Rather than functioning as just a charging station finder, EV Navigator aims to become a comprehensive digital companion for electric vehicle owners and people considering purchasing an EV.

## 2. Problem Statement

Electric vehicle owners and potential buyers face several challenges:

- Difficulty finding reliable and compatible charging stations.
- Uncertainty about the actual driving range and battery consumption of their vehicles.
- Difficulty planning long-distance journeys with suitable charging stops.
- Lack of personalized insights into real-world vehicle efficiency.
- Limited understanding of the financial benefits of switching from fuel vehicles to electric vehicles.
- Difficulty finding and comparing new and used electric vehicles.
- Lack of engaging tools to encourage efficient driving habits.

These challenges create an opportunity for an integrated EV platform that addresses the needs of both existing EV owners and potential EV buyers.

## 3. Proposed Solution

EV Navigator will provide an integrated platform that combines vehicle information, intelligent navigation, charging services, AI-powered insights and EV-related services.

The application will collect vehicle and journey information, process it to generate useful insights and help users make better decisions about driving, charging and EV ownership.

The platform will initially focus on Sri Lanka, considering local driving conditions, electricity tariffs, fuel prices, charging infrastructure and vehicle availability.

## 4. Core Product Pillars

### 4.1 Intelligent EV Navigation

- EV-specific route planning.
- Estimated battery consumption for planned journeys.
- Predicted battery percentage at the destination.
- Charging station discovery and filtering.
- Charging-stop recommendations based on vehicle compatibility.
- Estimated journey cost and travel information.

### 4.2 Personal EV Intelligence – EV Snapshot AI

- Capture vehicle dashboard photographs.
- AI-assisted extraction of battery percentage, displayed range and odometer readings.
- Allow users to review and confirm extracted information.
- Maintain historical vehicle readings.
- Record trip information and actual battery consumption.
- Analyze real-world vehicle efficiency.
- Use historical information to improve future energy predictions.

### 4.3 Eco-Driving and Rewards

- Calculate driving-efficiency scores.
- Award points, stars and badges.
- Introduce achievement levels and milestones.
- Encourage energy-efficient and safe driving.
- Introduce challenges and leaderboards in future releases.

### 4.4 EV Marketplace

- Discover new and used electric vehicles.
- Search and filter vehicles by brand, model and price.
- View vehicle specifications and battery information.
- View vehicle photographs and seller details.
- Save favourite vehicle listings.
- Introduce seller accounts and advanced listing management in future releases.

### 4.5 AI EV Assistant

- Answer EV-related questions.
- Explain vehicle battery and efficiency information.
- Assist with trip planning.
- Provide charging station recommendations.
- Explain vehicle-specific insights using available application data.
- Support natural-language interaction.

AI-generated recommendations must be grounded in verified application data and clearly distinguish estimates from confirmed information.

### 4.6 EV Savings Intelligence – EV vs Fuel Vehicle Comparison

- Compare electric vehicles with petrol and diesel vehicles.
- Calculate estimated running cost per kilometre.
- Compare daily, monthly and annual operating costs.
- Estimate potential savings from switching to an EV.
- Compare home charging and public charging costs.
- Estimate maintenance and ownership expenses.
- Calculate long-term total cost of ownership.
- Estimate the break-even period based on purchase-price differences.

Calculations will use configurable and date-stamped Sri Lankan electricity tariffs and fuel prices.

## 5. Target Audience

- Existing electric vehicle owners in Sri Lanka.
- People planning long-distance journeys using electric vehicles.
- People interested in understanding vehicle battery performance.
- Individuals considering purchasing an electric vehicle.
- People interested in comparing EVs with conventional fuel vehicles.
- EV enthusiasts interested in energy efficiency and vehicle technology.

## 6. Differentiation Strategy

EV Navigator aims to differentiate itself through an integrated, data-driven EV ownership experience.

Its long-term focus is personalized vehicle intelligence. By combining confirmed dashboard readings, trip distances and charging records, the platform aims to learn each vehicle's real-world energy behaviour and improve future battery-consumption predictions.

Additional differentiation opportunities include:

- Localized intelligence for Sri Lankan driving conditions.
- Personalized battery and efficiency analytics.
- Integration of trip planning and charging information.
- Financial comparisons between electric and fuel vehicles.
- Eco-driving engagement and rewards.
- A connected EV ownership and marketplace ecosystem.

The differentiation strategy will be validated through competitor research and feedback from actual EV owners.

## 7. MVP – Minimum Viable Product

The first release will focus on delivering a reliable, useful core experience.

### Included in the MVP

1. User registration and authentication.
2. User profiles and EV vehicle management.
3. Charging station discovery and filtering.
4. Basic EV-aware trip planning.
5. Estimated battery consumption and arrival battery percentage.
6. Charging-stop recommendations.
7. AI-assisted dashboard photo scanning with user confirmation.
8. Historical dashboard readings and basic trip records.
9. Basic vehicle-efficiency analytics.
10. EV versus petrol/diesel running-cost comparison.
11. A limited AI assistant connected to application data.

### Future Releases

- Advanced eco-driving points, badges and rewards.
- Public leaderboards and driving challenges.
- Complete EV marketplace with seller management.
- Advanced vehicle battery analytics.
- OBD-II and automatic vehicle telemetry integration.
- Charging reservations and integrated payments.
- Advanced AI-powered vehicle recommendations.

## 8. Technology Direction

The proposed technology stack is:

- **Mobile Application:** React Native, Expo and TypeScript.
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js and TypeScript.
- **Database:** PostgreSQL with PostGIS.
- **ORM:** Prisma.
- **Maps and Routing:** A suitable mapping and routing API.
- **AI Integration:** OpenAI API through the backend.
- **Deployment:** Docker and cloud infrastructure.
- **Version Control:** Git and GitHub.

The architecture will be modular to support future expansion.

## 9. Product Principles

- Accuracy and reliability over unnecessary complexity.
- Transparent battery and cost predictions.
- Verified and traceable charging information.
- User confirmation of AI-extracted vehicle readings.
- Privacy-conscious data collection.
- Safe and responsible driving incentives.
- Maintainable, scalable and modular software architecture.
- Continuous improvement using real-world user feedback.

## 10. Long-Term Vision

To establish EV Navigator as a trusted intelligent electric mobility ecosystem that supports the complete electric vehicle ownership journey.

From researching and purchasing an EV to planning journeys, discovering charging stations, understanding battery performance, managing ownership costs and improving driving efficiency, EV Navigator aims to bring these experiences together in one platform.
