# EV Navigator – Competitor Analysis

## 1. Introduction

The purpose of this competitor analysis is to identify existing applications and platforms in the electric vehicle industry, understand their capabilities and identify opportunities for EV Navigator.

EV Navigator aims to become an integrated EV ecosystem, initially focusing on the Sri Lankan market.

The research considers both local and international competitors.

## 2. Competitor Overview

### 2.1 EZGo

Website: https://ezgo.space/

**Market:** Sri Lanka

**Main features:**
- EV charging station discovery.
- Verified charging station information.
- AI-oriented energy-efficient routing.
- Live traffic information.
- Fuel station and other point-of-interest discovery.
- Turn-by-turn navigation.

**Relevance to EV Navigator:**

EZGo is a direct competitor in EV navigation and charging discovery.

### 2.2 ChargeLK

Website: https://chargelk.com/

**Market:** Sri Lanka

**Main features:**
- EV charging station access.
- QR-based charging sessions.
- Digital wallet and payments.
- Real-time charging cost information.
- Charging session history and receipts.

**Relevance to EV Navigator:**

ChargeLK focuses on charging operations and payments. It demonstrates the importance of integrating practical charging services into the EV experience.

### 2.3 ChargePath

Website: https://www.chargepath.lk/

**Market:** Sri Lanka

**Main features:**
- Charging station discovery.
- Connector and charging-speed filters.
- Advertised real-time charger availability.
- Battery-aware route planning.
- Charging-stop recommendations.
- Wallet and payment functionality.

**Relevance to EV Navigator:**

ChargePath overlaps with some of our planned navigation and charging features. We need to investigate its actual route prediction accuracy and coverage before defining our competitive advantage.

### 2.4 EVCircle

Website: https://evcircle.lk/

**Market:** Sri Lanka

**Main features:**
- Live charging station map.
- Charging slot reservations.
- Real-time availability.
- Cashless payments.
- Charging history.
- Charger-owner dashboard and management.

**Relevance to EV Navigator:**

EVCircle demonstrates the value of connecting EV drivers and charging station owners.

### 2.5 PlugShare

Website: https://www.plugshare.com/

**Market:** International

**Main features:**
- Global charging station discovery.
- Community reviews and ratings.
- Charging station photographs.
- Connector compatibility filters.
- Trip planning.
- User-reported station information.

**Relevance to EV Navigator:**

PlugShare demonstrates how community contributions can improve charging information and help drivers make better decisions.

### 2.6 WattMate

Website: https://www.watt-mate.app/

**Market:** International

**Main features:**
- AI-assisted dashboard and charger photo extraction.
- Charging session records.
- Charging cost tracking.
- Real-world efficiency analysis.
- Battery capacity and degradation estimates.
- Historical vehicle insights.

**Relevance to EV Navigator:**

WattMate overlaps with our EV Snapshot AI concept. This means dashboard photo extraction alone cannot be considered our unique selling point.

We should focus on combining vehicle data with personalized journey predictions and other EV services.

## 4. Market Observations

The initial research reveals several important observations:

1. Sri Lanka already has multiple applications focused on charging infrastructure and charging operations.
2. Battery-aware route planning is already being advertised by local products.
3. International platforms demonstrate the importance of community-maintained charging information.
4. Dashboard photo extraction and charging analytics already exist in international applications.
5. Charging payments and reservations are important parts of the wider EV ecosystem.
6. There is an opportunity to investigate a more integrated experience that connects vehicle history, trip planning, ownership costs and EV discovery.

## 5. Potential Opportunities for EV Navigator

### 5.1 Personalized Vehicle Intelligence

Use confirmed dashboard readings, trip distances and charging records to learn the real-world energy efficiency of individual vehicles.

### 5.2 Sri Lankan EV Journey Intelligence

Improve trip predictions using locally relevant road conditions, elevation, traffic and vehicle-specific efficiency information.

### 5.3 EV Savings Intelligence

Compare electric vehicles with petrol and diesel vehicles using Sri Lankan fuel prices, electricity tariffs and user-specific driving distances.

### 5.4 Integrated EV Ownership Experience

Connect vehicle profiles, trip planning, charging information, efficiency analytics and future marketplace services.

### 5.5 Eco-Driving Engagement

Explore rewards, achievements and efficiency scores to encourage safe and efficient driving.

### 5.6 Community-Verified Charging Information

Allow users to report incorrect station information, charging problems and outdated availability.

## 6. Proposed Competitive Positioning

EV Navigator aims to position itself as a personalized EV intelligence and ownership platform rather than only a charging station finder.

Its long-term differentiation will come from connecting:

- Real-world vehicle data.
- Personalized energy predictions.
- Charging infrastructure information.
- Trip planning.
- EV ownership cost comparisons.
- Eco-driving engagement.
- EV marketplace services.

## 7. Research Limitations and Next Steps

This is an initial desk-research exercise based on publicly available product information.

The next research activities should include:

- Installing and testing relevant competitor applications.
- Comparing actual user journeys.
- Checking charging station coverage and data accuracy.
- Evaluating route-planning results using sample Sri Lankan journeys.
- Interviewing EV owners about their current problems.
- Investigating how users currently calculate EV running costs.
- Validating whether users would adopt an integrated platform.

## 8. Conclusion

The EV market already contains products addressing many individual problems that EV Navigator intends to solve.

Therefore, EV Navigator should not rely on claiming that individual features are entirely new.

Its opportunity is to develop a connected and personalized EV ownership experience, with a particular focus on Sri Lankan driving conditions, vehicle-specific intelligence and financial decision-making.

The competitor analysis will be updated as additional research and user validation are completed.
