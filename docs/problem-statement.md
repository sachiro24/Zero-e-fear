# ZERO FEAR – Problem Statement

## 1. Introduction

Electric vehicles are becoming an increasingly important part of the transportation landscape in Sri Lanka. However, EV owners may face difficulties accessing reliable information and managing different aspects of electric vehicle ownership.

ZERO FEAR aims to address these challenges through a connected personal EV intelligence platform.

## 2. Identified Problems

### 2.1 Range Anxiety and Journey Planning

EV owners may be uncertain about whether their vehicle has sufficient battery capacity to complete a journey.

Factors such as distance, driving conditions, weather, elevation and vehicle efficiency can affect energy consumption.

### 2.2 Charging Station Information

Finding suitable charging stations can be difficult when information about connector compatibility, charging power, pricing and availability is incomplete or distributed across different platforms.

### 2.3 Limited Personal Vehicle Insights

EV owners may not have a convenient way to maintain historical records of battery readings, driving journeys and vehicle efficiency.

### 2.4 Fragmented EV Information

Information about electric vehicles, charging infrastructure, vehicle specifications and EV-related services may be spread across different applications and websites.

### 2.5 Understanding Real-World Vehicle Performance

Manufacturer-provided specifications do not always reflect an individual owner's real-world driving experience.

Owners need ways to record actual observations and understand their vehicle's performance over time.

## 3. Proposed Solution

ZERO FEAR proposes a unified EV ecosystem platform that brings together:

- Personal vehicle management.
- AI-assisted dashboard reading extraction.
- Historical battery reading records.
- Charging station discovery.
- EV-focused journey planning.
- Basic vehicle efficiency insights.
- Electric vehicle marketplace.

## 4. Core Value Proposition

ZERO FEAR aims to help EV owners make more informed decisions through accessible vehicle information, personalised records and EV-focused planning tools.

## 5. Initial Market

The initial target market is electric vehicle owners in Sri Lanka.

The platform may expand to additional EV-related services and markets as the product develops.

## 6. Product Differentiation

ZERO FEAR aims to connect vehicle information, dashboard observations, charging discovery and journey planning within one platform.

Its AI-assisted dashboard scanning workflow is intended to make vehicle reading collection easier and support the development of personalised vehicle insights.

## 7. Conclusion

ZERO FEAR aims to reduce uncertainty in electric vehicle ownership by creating a connected and accessible EV intelligence ecosystem.
