# ZERO FEAR – Target Users and User Personas

## 1. Overview

ZERO FEAR initially targets electric vehicle owners in Sri Lanka.

The platform is designed to support users at different stages of EV ownership, from people considering an electric vehicle to existing EV owners.

## 2. Primary Target Users

### 2.1 Existing EV Owners

People who currently own or regularly use electric vehicles.

Their needs include:
- Monitoring vehicle battery readings.
- Recording driving journeys.
- Discovering compatible charging stations.
- Planning journeys based on available battery.
- Understanding real-world vehicle efficiency.
- Accessing EV-related services.

### 2.2 Prospective EV Buyers

People who are considering purchasing an electric vehicle.

Their needs include:
- Exploring available electric vehicle models.
- Comparing vehicle specifications.
- Understanding battery capacity and driving range.
- Discovering used and new EV listings.
- Understanding EV ownership costs.

## 3. Secondary Target Users

### 3.1 EV Sellers and Dealers

Businesses or individuals interested in listing electric vehicles for sale.

Potential needs:
- Publishing vehicle listings.
- Managing vehicle information.
- Providing photographs and specifications.
- Responding to interested buyers.

### 3.2 Charging Station Operators

Businesses that operate EV charging facilities.

Potential needs:
- Making charging locations discoverable.
- Providing connector and charging information.
- Sharing pricing and facility details.
- Updating availability where supported.

## 4. User Personas

### Persona 1 – Existing EV Owner

**Profile:** An individual who owns an electric vehicle and uses it regularly.

**Goals:**
- Monitor battery readings.
- Find nearby charging stations.
- Plan long-distance journeys.
- Understand vehicle efficiency.

**Challenges:**
- Uncertainty about remaining range.
- Difficulty finding compatible charging facilities.
- Lack of centralised vehicle records.

**ZERO FEAR value:** Personal EV intelligence, charging discovery and battery-aware journey planning.

### Persona 2 – Prospective EV Buyer

**Profile:** An individual researching electric vehicles before purchasing one.

**Goals:**
- Compare different EV models.
- Understand vehicle specifications.
- Explore new and used EVs.
- Estimate ownership costs.

**Challenges:**
- Information distributed across multiple sources.
- Difficulty comparing vehicle specifications.
- Uncertainty about EV ownership.

**ZERO FEAR value:** EV marketplace and vehicle information.

### Persona 3 – EV Seller

**Profile:** An individual or business interested in selling electric vehicles.

**Goals:**
- Advertise available vehicles.
- Reach potential EV buyers.
- Share vehicle specifications and photographs.

**Challenges:**
- Finding relevant EV buyers.
- Presenting vehicle information effectively.

**ZERO FEAR value:** A dedicated EV marketplace.

## 5. Initial Geographic Focus

The initial product focus is Sri Lanka.

The application should consider local requirements such as:
- Sri Lankan rupee pricing.
- Local charging infrastructure.
- Locally available EV models.
- Local journey and road conditions.

## 6. Future User Groups

As the ecosystem develops, ZERO FEAR may expand to support:
- EV fleet operators.
- Charging network operators.
- EV service centres.
- Automotive businesses.
- EV communities.

## 7. Conclusion

ZERO FEAR aims to serve the wider electric vehicle ecosystem, beginning with individual EV owners and gradually supporting additional EV-related businesses and services.
